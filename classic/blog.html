ESGLedger piloted with Cyberport's Spatial Data Office

With the support of Watsons Water, the Hong Kong Development Bureau & Cyberport, OneChain introduced our flagship Greentech product — ESGLedger. This innovative solution enables ESG data traceability, a validated reporting system, and plastic credit tokenization, all aimed at building a greener circular economy. We also had the opportunity to exchange ideas and explore collaborations with CSDI Lab and the Lands Department. This pilot marks a significant milestone as the first PoC of its kind in Hong Kong, and we're eager to demonstrate its potential to drive sustainability innovation and foster tangible impact.

Powering GBA Halal Certification Center on-chain

The Halal industry in Hong Kong is growing rapidly, and we're excited to share how OneChain is contributing to this important movement. Through CertLedger, we're empowering trusted organizations like the GBA Halal Certification Center (GHCC) to issue blockchain-based certificates — offering immutable proof of authenticity and validity. GHCC plays a critical role in ensuring all processes adhere to Shariah principles and Halal standards, building trust and credibility in the Hong Kong Halal industry. Proud to be part of an effort driving transparency, innovation, and growth in this dynamic and globally important sector.

Partnering with JobMatriz on resume verification

Starting 2025 with exciting news — on the first working day of the year, we announced a strategic partnership between OneChain and JobMatriz.
